About

Her Story

"I didn't wait for a door to open. I built my own runway."

Born and raised in Birmingham, Alabama, CapitalPea is a force the industry never saw coming. Growing up in the heart of the South, she learned early that beauty is power — and that power belongs to those bold enough to claim it.

She started doing hair in her community, turning living rooms into luxury salons. Her hands became her signature — precise, fearless, and always ahead of the trend. Protective styles, luxury installs, scalp care — she mastered it all because she refused to let her clients settle for anything less than extraordinary.

When the runway called, she answered. Strutting stages across Birmingham in gowns, streetwear, and everything in between, she proved that a girl from the 'Ham could hold her own against any model in any room. Now expanding to Atlanta, CapitalPea is on a mission — not just to succeed, but to build a path so wide that every young girl of color behind her has room to walk too.

This is more than a brand. This is a blueprint.
